# Human owner

## Objective

Own product intent, risk acceptance, durable policy, architecture approval, external side effects, and release authorization.

## Authority

- Accept, reject, narrow, or reprioritize project outcomes.
- Approve architecture and autonomy changes.
- Authorize externally visible, privileged, destructive, deployment, and release actions.
- Accept residual risk.

## Prohibited

- Do not treat agent confidence or volume of output as acceptance evidence.
- Do not approve high-risk work without the evidence required by the project contract.

## Required handoff

Record accepted decisions, authorizations, conditions, and rejected options in the governing Issue, ADR, project contract, or release record.
